Before you email

The questions I get most often, answered up front so you don't have to ask.

What happens on the discovery call?

Thirty minutes, free, no sales pitch. You describe the problem, I ask clarifying questions, we figure out if I'm the right fit. If I'm not, I try to point you at someone who is. If I am, I send a written proposal within two business days.

How do I pay?

Tier 1 neighborhood work: card, ACH, Venmo, Zelle, or check — invoice same day, due on receipt. Projects and retainers: Stripe invoicing, ACH, or wire. Net-15 for retainers, 50/50 milestone split for fixed-price projects, weekly invoicing for T&M.

Do you work nights, weekends, or emergencies?

Planned after-hours work by arrangement — usually at 1.5× the tier's hourly rate. Same-day emergency response for retainer clients is included up to the retainer's hour bank; non-retainer emergency calls are $250/hr with a 2-hour minimum.

Will you sign an NDA?

Mutual NDAs, yes. One-way NDAs that make me indemnify you for your own data handling, no — that's what the MSA is for. Happy to redline a draft; can also provide a standard mutual-NDA template that usually works for both sides.

Do you travel outside Austin?

Remote work: anywhere, any timezone. On-site: free within the Austin metro; IRS-standard mileage outside it; full per-diem + flights for engagements that need multi-day on-site presence. Central Texas cities (San Antonio, Waco, Temple, Killeen, San Marcos) are regular territory.

Do you subcontract?

Not by default. If a project needs specialists I don't have (licensed PE, specific legal counsel, specialized cabling at scale), I introduce you directly and step out of the middle — I don't mark up other people's work. For ongoing retainers with more volume than I can personally handle, we'll discuss before signing.

Insurance and certificate of insurance?

General liability coverage in force. E&O (professional liability) and cyber-liability coverage bound per engagement where scope requires. Certificate of insurance available on request from the carrier — usually 1-2 business days.

What if the project goes over scope?

Fixed-price engagements have a written scope and a written change-order process. Small in-flight changes under 10% of the original estimate are absorbed; larger changes get a written amendment you approve before I start them. You don't get surprise invoices.
